Welcome, I’m Drake Reece the author of this blog! I created Lez Be Honest to be a space on the internet where people are real, we’re honest, and we don’t pretend to be things we’re not. Social media is filled with celebrities with unattainable lifestyles, Amazon links no one actually cares about, and paid partnerships to empty your wallet. Authenticity in the world we live in is rare but greatly needed.

If you have it all together this may not be the blog for you but if you’re looking for stories that actually happened and a few lessons learned along the way this is the place for you. Peace, kindness, and loving yourself are some of the top priorities around here. We don’t settle for things we don’t deserve, we live our lives, and most importantly we don’t have any regrets!

Lez Be Honest: A place where people are people and we do judge.
