At the risk of sounding like a motivational Instagram reel, I say live. When the idea of a trip pops into your head, take it. When you think of someone, tell them. Life isn’t fair! You don’t know when yours will end.

One day, you will (hopefully) be old, looking back at your life. What will you remember or be remembered by? Did you live in the same city for 80 years? Did you see all the places you wanted to see? Did you tell the people around you that you loved them?

There’s nothing wrong with living in the same city your entire life if that’s what you want for yourself. There’s nothing wrong with going on the same vacations every year. Unless it isn’t. If you’ve always lived in the same area out of convenience or because there’s “never a right time”, are you truly living the life you want to live? Most of the excuses we hide behind are stupid and don’t even matter.

> There are a world of excuses out there to not do something you love. Too old, too fat, too far away, too expensive, too whatever. There comes a day when all of that is true, and it’s too late.

If someone you don’t see yourself talking to in 10 years is holding you back, let them go! If it’s finances, restructure how you spend your money. Hot take: most people have the money to travel or do the things they want to do if they stopped going through drive-thrus and spent their money better on the things that matter more to them. Life doesn’t stop. Even if you say pretty please. Trust me, I’ve tried.

You’ve got to get out there and do what you love. Live like you don’t have a tomorrow. Pay off your debt, spend time outside, take pictures of pretty things! Don’t waste your life on things that don't serve you! If you leave spending time with your friends feeling bad about yourself, cya. If you’re spending time with family because of obligation, not because you genuinely want to spend time with them, we out!

> Life is far too short to waste it on people who don’t value you, don’t make you the best version of you, or don’t push you towards your goals!

If you’ve made it this far and feel the pressure to start doing things differently, do it! Don’t go on feeling like you’re settling into a comfortable, easy life if there is something better out there for you! As long as your heart is beating and there is breath in your lungs, there is time to do things differently. I’m not asking you to uproot your entire life, but **live life on your terms.**

Don’t let others pre-decide what you get to do in life. There will always be Monday, tomorrow, next week, next time, next year, next whatever until there’s not. Until one day you’re surrounded by the people you love for the last time as your heart stops. Then, it’s too late. Every day, people die. There are no guarantees that it won’t be you or me in that hospital bed tomorrow.

Live intentionally, live the life you want to live, and most of all, don’t have any regrets.
